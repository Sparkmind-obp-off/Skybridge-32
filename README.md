# Skybridge 32

**Personal 5K Race Coach** — a local-first PWA for preparing for Skybridge Run 2026.

## Mission

Help Haidar (BIB #6) arrive at race day prepared enough to complete the 5K within the organizer's official cutoff, with a calm and sustainable approach.

**Priority:** finish first, comfort second, time third.

## Race profile

- Event: Skybridge Run 2026 — Purwokerto
- Category: 5K
- BIB: #6 — Haidar
- Race date: 8 November 2026
- Preparation window: 7 October–8 November 2026
- Official 5K COT: 90 minutes (maximum completion window, not a training target)

## V0 philosophy

Skybridge 32 is not a Strava/Garmin clone and is not a medical product. It is a focused personal coach that answers one question each day:

> **What should I do today?**

V0 is local-first, offline-capable, mobile-first, and requires no account, backend, or third-party fitness API.

## Documentation

See the `docs/` directory for the product vision, PRD, training engine, UX, architecture, data model, race-day playbook, roadmap, external stack, implementation prompt, naming, and stakeholder assumptions.

## Safety

Training recommendations are conservative and should not override medical advice or official organizer instructions. Stop exercise and seek appropriate medical attention for concerning symptoms such as chest pain, fainting, severe shortness of breath, or unusual acute pain.
