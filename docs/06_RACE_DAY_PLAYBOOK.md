# Skybridge 32 — Race Day Playbook

## Official constraints

The organizer's current official instructions are authoritative.

Known working profile:

- 5K
- COT: 90 minutes
- Start: approximately 05:45 WIB
- BIB: #6

If organizer information changes, update the app rather than relying on this document.

## Before race day

Prepare:

- BIB/race pack;
- running shoes already tested in training;
- socks;
- comfortable clothes;
- phone;
- ID if required;
- transport plan;
- hydration plan;
- wake-up time;
- route to venue.

Do not use race day to test brand-new shoes, nutrition, or aggressive pacing.

## Start strategy

Do not chase faster runners.

Start conservatively.

Use the run/walk rhythm that was actually rehearsed.

## During race

Principles:

1. Keep effort controlled early.
2. Walk when the plan says walk.
3. Drink according to personal tolerance and organizer provisions.
4. If the body feels wrong, slow down or stop.
5. Follow marshals and organizer instructions.

## COT

90 minutes is the maximum completion window.

It is not a goal.

The app should not encourage a runner to intentionally average 18:00/km simply because that is the cutoff.

## Race mode

Race Mode should minimize interaction:

- start;
- current phase;
- elapsed time;
- optional manual checkpoint;
- hydration cue;
- finish.

Avoid unnecessary screens.

## Medical/safety rule

If the runner develops concerning symptoms such as chest pain, fainting, severe unusual shortness of breath, confusion, or acute severe pain, stop and seek appropriate medical assistance.

Do not try to "beat the timer" through a medical warning.

## Finish

After finishing:

- stop the timer;
- record the result;
- drink/eat according to tolerance;
- cool down gently if comfortable;
- celebrate completion.

The race is the experience, not merely the number on the clock.
