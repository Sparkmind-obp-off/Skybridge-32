# Skybridge 32 — Master Vision

## 1. Product identity

**Name:** Skybridge 32  
**Descriptor:** Personal 5K Race Coach

Skybridge 32 is a 32-day personal preparation system for a first-time 5K participant. It is designed around one real user, one race, one deadline, and one practical outcome: arrive prepared enough to finish.

## 2. User and race

- Runner: Haidar
- BIB: #6
- Distance: 5K
- Event: Skybridge Run 2026, Purwokerto
- Race date: 8 November 2026
- Preparation starts: 7 October 2026
- Official 5K COT: 90 minutes

The COT is a hard event constraint, not a performance goal.

## 3. Current starting point

The runner is a beginner. Current activity is mostly walking/semi-jogging around 1–2 km, approximately 9–10 min/km, with stops when motivation or stamina drops.

The product must therefore optimize for:

1. consistency;
2. time on feet;
3. gradual tolerance;
4. confidence;
5. race-specific rehearsal.

It must not assume an athletic baseline.

## 4. Outcome hierarchy

1. Finish the 5K within official COT.
2. Finish feeling reasonably okay and without reckless effort.
3. Improve time only if it happens naturally.

A slower run/walk finish is preferable to an aggressive attempt that causes injury or exhaustion.

## 5. Product principles

### Finish first

Every recommendation is judged against successful completion, not leaderboard performance.

### Reduce decision fatigue

The app should tell the user what today's session is, why it matters, and how to start it.

### Run/walk is legitimate

Run/walk is a first-class strategy, not a fallback or failure state.

### Consistency beats heroics

Missing one session does not justify doubling the next session.

### The app is a brake

If recent load, soreness, or effort indicates excessive stress, the app should recommend easier work or rest.

### Real data beats assumptions

Training ratios should adapt from actual sessions rather than being permanently hard-coded.

### Local-first privacy

V0 keeps training logs on the device. No account or server-side health/activity store is required.

## 6. Non-goals

V0 is not:

- a social network;
- a leaderboard;
- a medical diagnosis tool;
- a GPS navigation system;
- a wearable replacement;
- a full Strava/Garmin competitor;
- an AI chatbot;
- a subscription fitness platform.

## 7. Success criteria

The product succeeds if, before race day, the user can:

- understand today's training without planning it manually;
- complete several consistent sessions;
- tolerate progressively longer easy sessions;
- identify a comfortable run/walk rhythm;
- rehearse a race strategy;
- complete an appropriate 5K rehearsal if readiness supports it;
- arrive at the start with a simple plan and checklist.

## 8. Product mantra

> **32 days. One race. One goal: finish well.**
